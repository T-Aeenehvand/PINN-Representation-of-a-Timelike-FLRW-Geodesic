# Provenance statement

The principal numerical evidence consists of the six saved seed-42 checkpoints and their associated configurations, histories, validation arrays, and audits. The repository preserves these artifacts under `results/archived_seed42/six_cell_run/`.

The cleaned source tree is an organizational and reproducibility layer. It does not overwrite the archived model parameters. The supplementary Cartesian runs are separate fresh executions and are reported as such.
